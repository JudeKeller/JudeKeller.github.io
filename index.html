About

I am a 1st year Masters student at Penn State studying metabolomics and plant physiology in the genus Mentha.

I am interested in the controlled environment agriculture and the opportunities it provides us for micromanaging the growing environment to yield higher value crops.

Outside of the lab, I am an avid baker, coffee enthusiast, and cyclist.
